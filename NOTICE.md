# What the licence covers

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code, tools, documentation and artwork written for this
port, with these exceptions:

- **`mods/`**: edited versions of functions from the game, written against the
  [Concrete Jungle decompilation](https://github.com/Adolini1715/Concrete-Jungle-Decomp). They are
  reconstructed from the game's own code and carry no licence from this project; see that
  repository's `NOTICE.md`.
- **`packs/sharp-ui/`**: redraws of the game's front-end art. The fonts are rebuilt from Liberation
  Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1); the frames and buttons are traced from the game's art, which
  remains its owners'. Offered for use with your own copy of the game.
- **`patches/xboxrecomp-v0.15.0-pcj.patch`**: changes to
  [xboxrecomp](https://github.com/sp00nznet/xboxrecomp), which is MIT-licensed by its authors.
- **`third_party/`**: stb (public domain or MIT) and openlibm (see `third_party/openlibm-cj/LICENSE.md`),
  under their own licences.

No part of the game (code, data, art, audio or video) is in this repository. Building the port
needs your own copy of Predator: Concrete Jungle.
